## What step 7 wrote

### why_in_hospital  (from: sections.0, sections.1)

You came in with a serious infection in the skin and soft tissue of your left thigh. This is called a necrotising soft-tissue infection. The infection made you very unwell and caused septic shock, which means your body's organs needed support to keep working. You also developed:
- A lung condition called ARDS, which made breathing very difficult.
- A kidney problem (AKI) that needed a special kidney-support machine.
- Weakness from being in intensive care.
- A new irregular heart rhythm called atrial fibrillation (AF).

### what_we_did  (from: sections.2, sections.3)

Tests showed the infection was caused by two types of bacteria: Group A Streptococcus and anaerobes.

To treat you, the team:
- Put you on a breathing machine and gave you medicines to support your heart and blood pressure.
- Took you to theatre four times (on days 0, 2, 5 and 9) to clean and remove the infected tissue. The wound was then closed and a special wound dressing (VAC dressing) was used.
- Gave you strong antibiotics through a drip, which were later changed to the best ones for your infection.
- Turned you face-down twice to help your lungs recover.
- Supported your kidneys with a machine until day 16, when your kidneys recovered on their own.
- Fed you through a tube into your stomach until you were able to eat and drink again.
- Removed and replaced drip lines when a line infection was found on day 14.
- Took the breathing tube out on day 18 and moved you to the ward on day 24.
- Arranged district nurses and a tissue viability team to help your wound heal.
- Started you on physiotherapy and rehabilitation to help you regain your strength.
