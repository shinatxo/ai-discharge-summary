## What step 7 wrote

### why_in_hospital  (from: sections.0, sections.1)

Your child came in because they had a runny nose, a worsening cough, noisy breathing and was not feeding well. They were diagnosed with bronchiolitis. This is a common chest infection in young babies caused by a virus.

### what_we_did  (from: sections.2, sections.3)

We took a swab from the back of your child's nose. This confirmed the virus causing the infection was RSV (respiratory syncytial virus).

When your child arrived, their breathing was fast and their oxygen levels were low. We gave them extra oxygen to help with this. By Day 2 they no longer needed extra oxygen.

Because your child was not feeding enough, we passed a small tube through their nose into their stomach to give them feeds. By Day 2 they were feeding much better on their own, so the tube was removed.

Bronchiolitis is caused by a virus, so antibiotics were not needed.
