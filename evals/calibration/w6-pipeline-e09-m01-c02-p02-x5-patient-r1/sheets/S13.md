## What step 7 wrote

### why_in_hospital  (from: sections.0, sections.1)

You came in with a severe infection of the skin and tissue in your left thigh. This is called a necrotising soft-tissue infection. The infection caused your body to go into septic shock, which means your organs were under serious strain.

### what_we_did  (from: sections.2, sections.3)

Tests showed the infection was caused by two types of bacteria: Group A Streptococcus and anaerobes.

To support your body, the team:
- Put a breathing tube in and used a machine to help you breathe.
- Gave you medicines to support your blood pressure.
- Used a machine to support your kidneys (CVVH). Your kidney function recovered and the machine was stopped on day 16.

You had four operations to clean the infected tissue (on days 0, 2, 5 and 9), followed by a washout, closure of the wound, and a special wound dressing (VAC dressing).

You were given a long course of antibiotics through a drip (piperacillin-tazobactam), then changed to two different antibiotics (benzylpenicillin and clindamycin) based on advice from the microbiology team.

You were turned face-down twice to help your lungs (a treatment called proning).

You were fed through a tube into your stomach, and then moved on to eating and drinking by mouth.

The breathing tube was removed on day 18. You moved from intensive care to a ward on day 24.

Your wound is healing, with support from district nurses and the tissue viability team. You have also been having rehabilitation and physiotherapy.
