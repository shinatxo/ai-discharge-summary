Your child was referred in by their GP. They had a runny nose, a worsening cough, noisy breathing, and was not feeding well. They were also having fewer wet nappies.

The team found that your child had bronchiolitis (a chest infection caused by a virus). A swab from the nose and throat showed the virus was RSV. On arrival, your child's breathing was fast and they needed extra oxygen.

### what_we_did  (from: sections.2, sections.3)

No antibiotics were given, as the illness was caused by a virus. As your child improved, the extra oxygen was stopped. The feeding tube was removed as your child began to take full feeds by mouth. By day 2, breathing had settled and oxygen levels were stable.
