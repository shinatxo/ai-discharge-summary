## What step 7 wrote

### why_in_hospital  (from: sections.0, sections.1)

You came into hospital with a serious infection of the soft tissue in your left thigh. This is called a necrotising soft-tissue infection. It caused septic shock.

During your stay, the team also found and treated:
- ARDS (a serious condition affecting your lungs)
- Acute kidney injury
- Weakness picked up in the ICU
- An infection in one of your lines on day 14
- New atrial fibrillation (an irregular heart rhythm)

### what_we_did  (from: sections.2, sections.3)

Tests on the wound found two types of bacteria:
- Group A Streptococcus
- Anaerobes

To support your body, the team:
- Put a breathing tube in and used a machine to help you breathe
- Gave you medicines to support your blood pressure
- Used a kidney support machine (CVVH) until your kidneys recovered on day 16
- Turned you face-down twice to help your lungs
- Changed your lines on day 14 because of the line infection
- Fed you through a tube into your stomach, then moved on to eating and drinking by mouth

For the infection in your thigh, you had four operations to clean the wound on days 0, 2, 5 and 9. After that, the wound was washed out, closed, and a wound dressing (VAC dressing) was used. Your wound is now healing, with support from the district nurse and tissue viability team.

You had a long course of antibiotics given into a vein. These were first an antibiotic (piperacillin-tazobactam), then changed to two antibiotics (benzylpenicillin and clindamycin) based on advice from the microbiology team.

You were breathing on your own from day 18. You moved to the ward on day 24.

You had a prolonged period of rehabilitation and physiotherapy.

- Wound care — new medicine
