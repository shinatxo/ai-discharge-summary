When you arrived, your oxygen levels were low and your lungs were not clearing carbon dioxide properly. This is called type 2 respiratory failure.

---

## What we did to help you

- We gave you **controlled oxygen** to bring your oxygen levels up safely.
- We used a **breathing support mask (non-invasive ventilation, or NIV)** to help your lungs. You used this from when you arrived until day three of your stay, when your breathing had improved enough to stop it.
- We gave you **nebulisers** — medicines breathed in as a fine mist — to open up your airways.
- We gave you **steroid medicine (hydrocortisone)** through a drip at first, then as tablets (prednisolone) to reduce inflammation in your lungs.
- We gave you **antibiotic medicine (amoxicillin)** through a drip at first, then as tablets, to treat the chest infection.
- A nurse or clinician checked that you are using your **inhalers** in the best way.
- We talked with you about **smoking**.
- We gave you a **rescue pack** to take home (see below).

---
