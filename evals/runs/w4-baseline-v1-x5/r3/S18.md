The team ran some tests, including blood tests and a scan of your brain (CT head). These all came back normal. You were seen by the neurology (brain and nerve) team, who reviewed you before you went home.

You will also be given an appointment for a **first-fit clinic**, where the neurology team will go through all your results with you and decide on next steps.
