## What was wrong

Your baby had a chest infection called **bronchiolitis**. This is caused by a virus called **RSV (respiratory syncytial virus)**. It made his airways swell and fill with mucus, which caused:

- a noisy, wheezy cough
- fast or difficult breathing
- poor feeding
- fewer wet nappies than usual

Bronchiolitis is very common in babies. A test on a swab from his nose confirmed the RSV virus.

---

## What we did in hospital

When your baby came in, the team checked his breathing and the level of oxygen in his blood. His oxygen level was a little low, so we gave him some extra oxygen through a small tube near his nose. By the next morning he was breathing well enough on his own without any extra oxygen.

Because he was not feeding well enough, we passed a thin, soft tube through his nose into his stomach (a **nasogastric tube**) to give him milk feeds. As his feeding improved — first to about three-quarters of his normal amount, then back to full feeds — the tube was taken out.

He was not given any antibiotics because bronchiolitis is caused by a virus, not bacteria. The team watched him closely throughout his stay.
