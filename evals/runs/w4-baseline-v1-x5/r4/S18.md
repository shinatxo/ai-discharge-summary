*This letter is for you to keep at home. It explains what happened, what was done, and what happens next. Please read it carefully.*

## What Happened

You were brought to hospital after having a seizure (a fit) at work. It was witnessed by others and lasted about 2 minutes. Afterwards you were briefly confused, which is normal after this type of seizure. You also had a small bite on the side of your tongue.

This type of seizure is called a **generalised tonic-clonic seizure**. Because this was your first one and there was no clear cause found, it is called a **first unprovoked seizure**.

---

## Tests We Did

You had blood tests, a blood sugar (glucose) check, and a drug screen. All of these came back normal. You also had a scan of your brain (CT head), which was also normal.

Two further tests have been arranged for you as an outpatient — more details are below.

---

## Treatment During Your Stay

The neurology (brain and nerve) team reviewed you during your stay. Because this was your first seizure and all your tests so far are normal, the team decided **not to start any seizure medicines yet**. The decision about longer-term treatment will be made after your outpatient tests and clinic appointment.

**Heights**
Do **not work at heights**.

**Baths**
Do **not have a bath on your own** (unsupervised).
