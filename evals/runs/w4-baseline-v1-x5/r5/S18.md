# Your Discharge Letter

**From the Neurology Team | Admitted: 19 May | Discharged: 20 May**

---

## What Happened

You were brought to hospital after having a seizure (a fit) at work. It was witnessed by others and lasted about 2 minutes. After the seizure you were briefly confused, which is normal. You also had a small bite on the side of your tongue.

This was your first seizure of this kind. Tests were done to help understand why it happened.

---

## Tests We Did

While you were in hospital, we checked your blood, including your blood sugar. All of these came back normal. A screen for drugs and toxins was also done and was negative. A scan of your brain (CT head) was normal too.

Two further tests still need to be done as an outpatient:

- A detailed brain scan (MRI brain)
- A brain-wave test (EEG)

You will be given appointments for both of these. The results will be looked at by the neurology team at your follow-up clinic.

---

## Treatment

You were seen by the neurology (brain and nerve) team during your stay. Because this was your first seizure and the immediate risk of another one was felt to be low, no seizure medicines were started. A decision about whether you need any medicine will be made after your scan and brain-wave test results are reviewed.

---

## Medicines

You were not taking any regular medicines before you came in, and you have not been given any new medicines to take home.

---

## What Happens Next

You have three outpatient appointments being arranged:

1. **MRI brain scan** — date to be confirmed
2. **EEG (brain-wave test)** — date to be confirmed
3. **First-fit clinic** (neurology outpatient appointment) — date to be confirmed

Your GP has been told about your admission. The neurology team will review your scan and test results at your clinic appointment.

If you do not hear about your appointment dates, please contact your GP surgery.

---

## Important Advice

The team has given you the following important safety advice. Please read this carefully.

**Driving**
You must **not drive**. You must tell the DVLA about your seizure. Driving is not allowed for **6 months from the date of your seizure (19 May)**. This applies to a standard car or motorcycle licence (Group 1). It is your legal responsibility to inform the DVLA.

**Water safety**
- Do not swim alone.
- Do not have a bath on your own (unsupervised).

**Working at heights**
- Do not work at heights.

---

## If You Are Worried

If you become unwell or are worried about anything, contact your GP or call NHS 111. Call 999 if it is an emergency.
