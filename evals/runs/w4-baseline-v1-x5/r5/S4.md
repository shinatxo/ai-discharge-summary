Your baby boy (10 months old) was admitted to hospital because he had a chest infection called **bronchiolitis**. This is caused by a virus called **RSV (respiratory syncytial virus)**. It made his breathing fast and noisy, and he was not feeding well.

---

## What we did

When he came in, his breathing was working hard and his oxygen levels were a little low. We gave him:

- **Extra oxygen** through a small tube near his nose, to keep his oxygen levels safe
- **Tube feeds (nasogastric feeds)** — a small, soft tube passed through his nose into his tummy — to make sure he got enough milk while feeding was difficult

Over the course of his stay, he improved. His oxygen was slowly reduced and then stopped. The feeding tube was removed once he was taking around three-quarters of his normal feeds by mouth, and by the time he went home he was feeding fully again.

He did **not** need antibiotics, because bronchiolitis is caused by a virus, not bacteria. He did not need any other medicines either.
