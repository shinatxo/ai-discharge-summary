The doctors found that she has **Type 1 Diabetes** (Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus). This means her body is not making a hormone called insulin, which is needed to keep blood sugar at a safe level.

She was also very unwell because her blood sugar had gone very high and her blood had become too acidic. This is called **diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)**. This is why she needed to come into hospital straight away.

---

## What was done in hospital

- How to count carbohydrates in food
- How to recognise and treat low blood sugar (hypoglycaemia)
